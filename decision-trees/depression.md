# Decision Tree: Major Depressive Disorder (Adults)

**Sources**: NICE NG222, Cipriani et al. 2018 Lancet network meta-analysis (522 trials, 116,477 participants), Leichsenring et al. 2022 umbrella review, Cuijpers et al. 2025 CBT unified series.

## 1. Assessment
- Severity (PHQ-9 or equivalent) + functional impairment + risk (suicide/self-harm).
- Previous treatment response, comorbidities, patient preference, social determinants.

## 2. Less Severe (subthreshold / mild)
- **First-line**: Psychological/psychosocial interventions (guided self-help, individual or group CBT, behavioural activation, group exercise, mindfulness).
- Antidepressants: not routinely. Only if patient preference or history of response to moderate-severe episodes. Prefer SSRI with good efficacy/acceptability balance (sertraline, escitalopram).

## 3. More Severe (moderate to severe)
- **Preferred**: Combination of high-intensity individual CBT + SSRI.
- Alternatives: SSRI monotherapy or psychological monotherapy (CBT, BA, IPT).
- SSRI choice: Sertraline or escitalopram often optimal balance (Cipriani). Start low, titrate, early review (1–2 weeks) for activation/suicidality risk.
- Cuijpers 2025: CBT effect size g ≈ 0.84 vs controls.

## 4. Inadequate Response (4–6 weeks at adequate dose)
- Check adherence, dose optimisation, diagnosis, psychosocial factors.
- Switch to another SSRI, mirtazapine, or SNRI (venlafaxine).
- Or intensify/combine psychological treatment.

## 5. Treatment-Resistant (≥2 adequate trials)
- Augmentation with second-generation antipsychotic (network meta-analyses support aripiprazole, quetiapine etc.).
- ECT for severe, psychotic, or urgent response need.
- Specialist care, measurement-based outcomes.

## 6. Throughout
- Shared decision-making, side-effect monitoring, relapse prevention, functional recovery focus.
- Realistic expectations: overall SMD ~0.34–0.36 (Leichsenring); personalise and address comorbidities.
- Integration with syndrome analysis when cognitive deficits prominent.
