# Decision Tree: First-Episode / Schizophrenia-Spectrum Psychosis

**Sources**: NICE, Huhn et al. 2019 Lancet network meta-analysis (402 studies, ~53k participants), Leucht series (including long-term 2023).

## Acute Phase
- Offer oral antipsychotic + psychological interventions (individual CBT + family intervention where available).
- Antipsychotic selection: Evidence-balanced agents (e.g., risperidone, aripiprazole; olanzapine with metabolic caution). Clozapine reserved for treatment-resistant (gold standard).
- Huhn 2019: SMDs vs placebo range from −0.89 (clozapine) downward; differences largely gradual. Side-effect profile + patient preference decisive.

## At-Risk Mental State
- CBT ± family intervention. No routine antipsychotics.

## Long-Term / Maintenance
- Leucht 2023: Olanzapine often stronger efficacy but high weight gain — careful risk-benefit.
- Clozapine remains standard for treatment-resistant.
- Regular metabolic monitoring, functional recovery focus, psychosocial interventions.

## Key Principles
- Ceiling effect awareness.
- Measurement-based care.
- Integration of qualitative syndrome analysis for cognitive/functional profile and rehab planning.
